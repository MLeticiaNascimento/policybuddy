# 1. Introduction

GlobalTech Solutions recognizes remote work as an important component of modern business operations, enabling flexibility, collaboration, and productivity while supporting employees across different locations and regions.

Remote work environments introduce unique security challenges because employees may access corporate systems, confidential information, and business resources outside traditional office environments.

This Remote Work and Security Policy establishes the requirements and responsibilities necessary to ensure that employees working remotely maintain appropriate security practices and protect company information.

The objective of this policy is to balance employee flexibility with the organization's responsibility to protect:

- Corporate information.
- Customer data.
- Intellectual property.
- Business operations.
- Technology resources.

All employees, contractors, and third parties performing remote work activities on behalf of GlobalTech Solutions must comply with the requirements defined in this policy.

---

# 2. Policy Objective

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Establish security requirements for remote work activities.
- Protect confidential information outside corporate facilities.
- Define acceptable practices for home office environments.
- Reduce risks associated with remote access.
- Ensure secure handling of company equipment and information.
- Promote employee awareness of remote work security responsibilities.

Employees working remotely remain responsible for protecting company information with the same level of care required in corporate offices.

---

# 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees working from home.
- Hybrid employees.
- Contractors performing remote activities.
- Consultants accessing company resources remotely.
- Third parties authorized to access GlobalTech Solutions systems.

This policy covers:

- Corporate laptops.
- Mobile devices.
- Remote access systems.
- VPN connections.
- Cloud applications.
- Corporate communication tools.
- Printed documents.
- Confidential conversations.
- Home office environments.

---

# 4. Remote Work Requirements

Employees working remotely must maintain an environment that supports secure business operations.

Remote workers must:

- Protect company equipment.
- Prevent unauthorized access.
- Follow information security requirements.
- Use approved communication tools.
- Protect confidential discussions.
- Follow company policies regarding data handling.

Remote work does not reduce employee responsibility for protecting company information.

---

# 5. Home Office Security Requirements

Employees should establish a secure workspace when performing company activities remotely.

Recommended practices include:

- Working in a private area whenever possible.
- Preventing unauthorized individuals from viewing company information.
- Securing physical documents.
- Locking devices when unattended.
- Maintaining updated security software.

Employees should avoid working in locations where confidential information may be exposed.

Examples:

- Shared family spaces without privacy.
- Public areas with unauthorized viewers.
- Locations where conversations can be overheard.

---

# 6. Use of Corporate Equipment

GlobalTech Solutions provides approved equipment to support secure remote work.

Corporate devices include:

- Laptops.
- Mobile phones.
- Tablets.
- Other approved technology resources.

Employees must:

- Keep devices physically protected.
- Use company-approved security tools.
- Install required updates.
- Report problems immediately.
- Follow device management requirements.

Employees must not:

- Allow family members or unauthorized individuals to use corporate devices.
- Remove security software.
- Disable security controls.
- Modify system configurations without approval.

---

# 7. Personal Device Usage

The use of personal devices for company activities is restricted.

Employees may only use personal devices when:

- Explicitly authorized.
- Security requirements are met.
- Appropriate protection controls are enabled.

Employees must not store confidential company information on:

- Personal computers.
- Personal external drives.
- Personal cloud accounts.
- Unauthorized storage devices.

Example:

An employee downloads customer documents to a personal laptop because the company laptop is unavailable.

This action is prohibited because the information is outside company-controlled security environments.

---

# 8. Screen Protection and Workspace Privacy

Employees must protect company information displayed on screens.

Requirements include:

- Locking screens when away from devices.
- Positioning screens away from unauthorized viewers.
- Using privacy filters when required.
- Avoiding displaying confidential information in public spaces.

Employees must ensure that family members, visitors, or other individuals cannot access company information.

---

# 9. Handling Printed Documents

Printing confidential company information while working remotely should be avoided whenever possible.

If printing is necessary, employees must:

- Ensure documents are protected.
- Store documents securely.
- Prevent unauthorized access.
- Dispose of documents properly.

Employees must not:

- Leave confidential documents unattended.
- Throw sensitive documents into regular trash.
- Share printed materials with unauthorized individuals.

Confidential documents should be destroyed using approved methods.

---

# 10. Confidential Conversations

Employees must protect confidential business discussions when working remotely.

Employees should avoid discussing sensitive topics in:

- Public transportation.
- Restaurants.
- Cafes.
- Shared public areas.

Examples of confidential information:

- Customer details.
- Financial discussions.
- Business strategies.
- Employee information.
- Security incidents.

Employees should use private environments for confidential meetings whenever possible.

---

# 11. Public Wi-Fi Usage

Public wireless networks create additional security risks.

Employees must avoid accessing sensitive company resources through unsecured public Wi-Fi.

Examples:

- Airport networks.
- Hotel networks.
- Coffee shop Wi-Fi.
- Public hotspots.

When public networks must be used, employees must:

- Connect through approved VPN services.
- Avoid accessing unnecessary sensitive information.
- Follow company security requirements.

Employees must not:

- Disable VPN protections.
- Connect to unknown networks.
- Share company credentials over insecure connections.

---

# 12. VPN Requirements

Employees accessing internal company resources remotely must use approved VPN solutions when required.

VPN provides:

- Secure communication.
- Encrypted connections.
- Protection against unauthorized interception.

Employees must:

- Use company-provided VPN tools.
- Report VPN issues.
- Follow authentication requirements.

Employees must not:

- Share VPN credentials.
- Use unauthorized VPN services.
- Attempt to bypass VPN controls.

---

# 13. Remote Work Example Scenarios

# Case 1: Employee Working in a Coffee Shop

## Situation:

A GlobalTech Solutions employee works from a coffee shop using a corporate laptop to complete tasks involving internal documents.

## Risks:

Potential risks include:

- Other customers viewing confidential information.
- Conversations being overheard.
- Theft of corporate equipment.
- Connection to insecure networks.

## Applicable Policies:

- Remote Work and Security Policy.
- Information Security Policy.
- Data Protection and Privacy Policy.

## Recommended Actions:

The employee should:

- Use a privacy screen.
- Avoid displaying confidential documents publicly.
- Use VPN.
- Position the laptop to prevent unauthorized viewing.
- Avoid discussing confidential topics.

Working from public locations requires additional security awareness.

---

# Case 2: Employee Leaves Laptop Unattended

## Situation:

An employee leaves a corporate laptop unattended in a vehicle while attending a personal appointment.

## Risks:

- Device theft.
- Unauthorized access.
- Exposure of confidential information.

## Required Action:

Employees must keep corporate devices secure at all times.

If a device is lost or stolen, the employee must immediately report the incident to Information Security.

---

# Case 3: Family Member Uses Corporate Device

## Situation:

An employee allows a family member to use a company laptop for personal activities.

## Risks:

- Unauthorized access.
- Malware infection.
- Exposure of corporate information.

## Required Action:

Corporate devices must only be used by authorized employees.

---

# 14. Remote Access Security

Employees accessing company systems remotely must:

- Use approved authentication methods.
- Protect credentials.
- Follow access control requirements.
- Report suspicious activity.

Employees must not:

- Share login information.
- Store credentials insecurely.
- Access systems without authorization.

---

# 15. Security Incident Reporting

Employees must immediately report remote work security incidents.

Examples include:

- Lost or stolen devices.
- Accidental disclosure of information.
- Unauthorized access.
- Suspicious emails.
- Malware infections.
- Exposure of confidential documents.

Reporting channels:

**GlobalTech Security Portal**

**Email:**  
security.incident@globaltechsolutions.example

Early reporting helps reduce business impact and enables appropriate response actions.

# 16. Employee Responsibilities

Employees working remotely must:

- Follow security requirements.
- Protect company equipment.
- Maintain workspace privacy.
- Use approved tools.
- Report incidents promptly.
- Complete security awareness training.

Employees are responsible for ensuring their remote work environment supports secure business activities.

---

# 17. Manager Responsibilities

Managers must:

- Ensure employees understand remote work expectations.
- Support secure working practices.
- Approve remote access requirements when applicable.
- Address security concerns.

Managers must encourage employees to report security issues without fear of retaliation.

---

# 18. Human Resources Responsibilities

Human Resources is responsible for:

- Defining remote work guidelines.
- Supporting employee awareness.
- Managing remote work agreements.
- Coordinating with Security when necessary.

---

# 19. Information Security Responsibilities

Information Security is responsible for:

- Providing remote access controls.
- Monitoring security risks.
- Managing security incidents.
- Defining technical requirements.
- Supporting employees with security guidance.

---

# 20. Violations and Disciplinary Actions

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

- Security training requirements.
- Access restrictions.
- Formal warnings.
- Disciplinary actions.
- Termination of employment or contracts.

Examples of violations include:

- Sharing corporate devices.
- Storing confidential information improperly.
- Using unauthorized networks without protection.
- Ignoring security requirements.

The severity of actions depends on:

- Risk created.
- Impact of the incident.
- Employee intent.
- Previous violations.
