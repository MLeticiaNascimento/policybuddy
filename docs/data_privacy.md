# 1. Introduction

GlobalTech Solutions recognizes that protecting personal data and confidential information is essential to maintaining trust with customers, employees, business partners, and other stakeholders.

As a global organization operating in technology-driven and highly regulated environments, GlobalTech Solutions is committed to protecting information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, disclosure, or any form of inappropriate processing.

This Data Protection and Privacy Policy establishes the mandatory principles, requirements, responsibilities, and controls for the collection, storage, processing, sharing, and disposal of personal data and confidential information handled by GlobalTech Solutions.

All employees, contractors, service providers, suppliers, and authorized third parties who access company information must comply with the requirements established in this policy.

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary actions, access restrictions, contractual consequences, and other administrative measures according to company standards.

---

# 2. Policy Objective

The objective of this policy is to:

- Ensure that personal data is processed securely, ethically, and transparently.
- Establish requirements for preventing information leaks and unauthorized disclosure.
- Define employee responsibilities regarding data protection.
- Support compliance with applicable privacy laws and regulatory requirements.
- Reduce risks associated with improper handling of corporate information.
- Provide employees with clear guidelines for storing, sharing, and processing information.

GlobalTech Solutions follows the following fundamental data protection principles:

## Confidentiality

Information must only be accessed by authorized individuals with a legitimate business need.

## Integrity

Information must remain accurate, complete, and protected against unauthorized modification.

## Availability

Information must be accessible to authorized users whenever required for business operations.

## Data Minimization

Employees must only access, collect, and process information necessary to perform their assigned responsibilities.

# 4. Definitions

## 4.1 Personal Data

Personal data is any information related to an identified or identifiable individual.

Examples include:

- Full name.
- Address.
- Telephone number.
- Email address.
- Identification documents.
- Location information.
- Employment information.

Personal data must be handled according to applicable privacy requirements and company security standards.

---

## 4.2 Sensitive Personal Data

Sensitive personal data refers to information that requires additional protection due to its potential impact on an individual's privacy.

Examples include:

- Medical information.
- Biometric information.
- Health records.
- Genetic information.
- Racial or ethnic information.
- Religious or philosophical information.

Processing sensitive personal data requires appropriate justification, additional safeguards, and authorization when applicable.

---

## 4.3 Confidential Information

Confidential information refers to any information belonging to GlobalTech Solutions, customers, or business partners that is not publicly available.

Examples include:

- Business contracts.
- Financial information.
- Customer records.
- Internal strategies.
- Product plans.
- Proprietary source code.
- Security documentation.

Confidential information must only be accessed, used, and shared according to approved business requirements.

---

## 4.4 Data Controller

A data controller is an organization responsible for determining the purposes and methods of processing personal information.

GlobalTech Solutions may act as a data controller for certain internal and customer-related processes.

---

## 4.5 Data Processor

A data processor is an organization or individual that processes personal data on behalf of a data controller according to documented instructions.

Examples include:

- Technology providers.
- Cloud service providers.
- Outsourced processing companies.

Data processors must comply with contractual obligations and security requirements defined by GlobalTech Solutions.

# 5. Data Protection Requirements

# 5.1 Customer Data Sharing

Customer data may only be shared when:

- There is a legitimate business purpose.
- The sharing has been authorized.
- The recipient has appropriate access permissions.
- Required security controls are applied.

Employees are prohibited from:

- Sending customer information to unauthorized individuals.
- Sharing customer reports without approval.
- Copying customer data into personal environments.

### Example:

A customer support employee receives a request from another department requesting a spreadsheet containing customer information.

The employee must verify that the requester has proper authorization before sharing any data.

---

# 5.2 Use of External Tools

External applications and services may only be used for corporate information processing when:

- They have been approved by the appropriate department.
- Security requirements have been evaluated.
- Data protection controls are available.

Employees must not upload confidential company information into:

- Public websites.
- Free online tools without approval.
- Applications that have not completed security assessment.

### Example:

An employee uploads a customer database into an online analysis tool to generate a report.

This action is prohibited because the information may be stored or processed outside company-controlled environments.

---

# 5.3 Storage on Personal Devices

Storing corporate information on unauthorized personal devices is prohibited.

This includes:

- Personal computers.
- Personal external drives.
- Personal USB devices.
- Personal cloud storage accounts.

If business circumstances require temporary use of personal equipment, an exception request must be submitted and approved through the official process.

---

# 5.4 Corporate Email Usage

Corporate email must be used for professional communication related to company activities.

Employees must:

- Verify recipients before sending messages.
- Apply encryption when required.
- Protect confidential attachments.

Employees must not:

- Forward corporate documents to personal email accounts.
- Send confidential information to incorrect recipients.
- Use personal email accounts to store company information.

---

# 5.5 Messaging Applications

Personal messaging applications must not be used to share confidential corporate information.

Examples of prohibited activities:

- Sending customer contracts through personal messaging apps.
- Sharing screenshots of internal systems.
- Sending confidential documents through unauthorized platforms.

Employees must use approved corporate communication tools for business discussions.

---

# 5.6 Access Control

Access to information must follow the principle of least privilege.

Employees should only receive access required to perform their assigned responsibilities.

Managers are responsible for:

- Requesting appropriate access.
- Reviewing employee permissions.
- Removing unnecessary access after role changes.

Sharing user credentials is strictly prohibited.

---

# 5.7 Password Requirements

Employees must:

- Use individual passwords.
- Keep credentials confidential.
- Create strong passwords.
- Follow password management requirements.

Employees must not:

- Share passwords.
- Store passwords in publicly accessible locations.
- Reuse corporate passwords on personal websites.

---

# 5.8 Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled whenever available.

MFA provides additional identity verification through methods such as:

- Authentication applications.
- Security tokens.
- Additional verification steps.

Employees must not disable or bypass MFA security controls.

---

# 6. Common Policy Violations

## 6.1 Sending Customer Data to Personal Email

### Scenario:

An employee sends a customer spreadsheet to their personal email account to continue working from home.

### Risk:

- Loss of company control over information.
- Potential exposure of personal data.
- Increased risk of unauthorized access.

### Correct Action:

Employees must use approved corporate systems for remote work.

---

## 6.2 Sharing Confidential Files Through WhatsApp

### Scenario:

An employee sends a confidential contract to a supplier through a personal messaging application.

### Risk:

- Lack of corporate monitoring.
- Unauthorized storage.
- Potential data exposure.

### Correct Action:

Use approved corporate collaboration tools.

---

## 6.3 Uploading Information to Public AI Tools

### Scenario:

An employee copies customer information into a public artificial intelligence tool to generate a summary.

### Risk:

- Confidential information exposure.
- Unauthorized processing.
- Loss of data governance control.

### Correct Action:

Only company-approved AI solutions may be used for corporate information processing.

---

## 6.4 Public Exposure of Confidential Documents

### Scenario:

An employee accidentally uploads an internal document to a public file-sharing platform.

### Required Action:

The incident must be immediately reported to the Information Security team.

Employees must not attempt to hide or delay reporting security incidents.

---
